A few strokes, unmistakably Qatari.

  1. 1One light line: 1.8px at 64px, round caps, drawn by hand. Scale the whole icon, never the stroke alone.
  2. 2One colour each: Fahem only blue, Wasel only orange. The only fill is the white of the ghutra, on a soft tile (#F1EEE8, or Mist and Apricot in scenes).
  3. 3Headwear is the signature: Fahem is Qatari, with a white ghutra in a soft dome, the face opening rising to the sharp "cobra" point, and the agal's double cord on the crown. Wasel is Omani, with the embroidered kumma cap: a short round cap with a row of dots and a row of rings. Never a red check (Saudi) or an agal-less wrap (Emirati).
  4. 4Heads only: a face shape, headwear, eyes, brows, nose and mouth. No bodies, clothes, beards, props or accessories.
  5. 5Warm, never eerie: eyes always have solid pupils, brows carry the expression, and there are no jaw or skull outlines inside a framed face. Fahem's eyes smile; Wasel winks. Both are clean-shaven, so the headwear tells them apart. Two neighbours, one Gulf.
  6. 6Motion is small: blink, nod, lean, a card sliding, a thread drawing. 6–8 second loops, and still for reduced motion.
